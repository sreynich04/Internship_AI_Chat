School of Graduate Studies
The School of Graduate Studies (SGS) at CamTech University has a philosophy deeply rooted in a proactive response to the dynamic challenges facing our world today, particularly in the context of Industry Revolution 4.0 and its environmental and social implications. Positioned strategically, SGS aims to address these complexities through its overarching theme: “Science, Technology, and Sustainability (STS).” At the heart of SGS’s philosophy are three foundational pillars: academic excellence, impacts on career, and community engagement. These pillars serve as guiding principles shaping the core curriculum across all majors, embodying a holistic approach to graduate education. The vision of becoming a leading graduate school in STS is intricately woven into the design of the core curriculum, aligning educational objectives with the broader goal of producing skilled graduates capable of addressing complex issues in sustainable development. The goals and objectives further solidify this philosophy, emphasizing the establishment of SGS as a center for interdisciplinary collaboration and engagement. By offering high-quality master’s and doctoral programs, fostering diverse perspectives, and prioritizing critical thinking and problem-solving skills, the core curriculum reflects a commitment to nurturing well-rounded individuals poised to make significant contributions in their respective fields.

Teaching and Learning Philosophy
SGS understands the dynamic educational landscape of the 21st century and tailors our curriculum to suit the needs of our graduate students. We place great emphasis on adult education and employ innovative delivery methods, including flipped learning and blended learning methods. Our teaching and learning principles strongly encourage:
Involvement: Active participation in planning and evaluation processes.
Past Experiences: Leverage previous successes and mistakes for future learning.
Relevant Topics: Emphasize immediate relevance and impact.
Problem-Centric Learning: Prioritize practical problem-solving over memorization.

Admission Requirements
Students can be admitted only if they meet all of the following conditions.
Hold a bachelor’s degree with a minimum GPA of 3.20 from an accredited educational institution in Cambodia or overseas;
Have an English language proficiency score that meets one of the following tests below
IELTS with a minimum of 6.0; the writing band must not be less than 6
TOEFL iBT with a minimum of 60
TOEFL PBT with a minimum of 500
CEFE with a minimum of B2;
Have a research proposal that can be turned into a thesis;
Have a proven track record of publications (if any);
Submit two letters of recommendation. At least one of them must come from a university professor.
Noteworthy achievements that could contribute to a Recognition of Prior Learning assessment (if any).
Hold a master’s degree with a minimum GPA of 3.50 from an accredited educational institution in Cambodia or overseas;
IELTS with a minimum of 6.5 with no individual band less than 6
TOEFL iBT with a minimum of 80
TOEFL PBT with a minimum of 550
CEFE with a minimum of C1;
Have a strong research proposal that can be turned into a doctoral dissertation;
Have a proven track record of publications (if any)
Be accepted by a CamTech doctoral supervisor who will be designated as the student’s principal supervisor;
Submit two letters of recommendation. At least one of them must come from a university professor;
Program Schedule
The program follows the trimester system, where the academic year is divided
into three terms. Each term lasts at least 15 weeks. In general, coursework takes up
the first year. Students then take around six additional terms to work on their
thesis and journal publication.
In terms of study schedule, the graduate students are expected to study on
Friday 1:00 pm – 3:00 pm and 3:30 pm – 5:30 pm
Saturday 8:00 am – 12:30 pm and 1:00 pm: 3:30 pm

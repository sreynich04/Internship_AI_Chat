In an era defined by artificial intelligence, CamTech University emerges as a bold force for transformation — Cambodia’s first AI-driven university and a trailblazer in the region.
At CamTech, AI is our foundation. We are the first in Cambodia to offer a complete academic pathway in
AI — from bachelor’s
to PhD — taught by dedicated, full-time faculty who combine deep technical expertise with a passion for teaching and discovery.
But we go further.
CamTech is the first university in Asia to offer a
PhD in Science, Technology, and Society (STS)
— a groundbreaking program that prepares students to lead at the intersection of innovation, ethics, and social change.
CamTech hosts three distinct but complementary initiatives that support education,
research
, and values-based innovation in artificial intelligence. The
AI Forum (AIF)
is a pioneering platform founded in 2018 to promote critical dialogue on AI’s societal impact.
The Center for Buddhist AI Ethics
brings ancient wisdom into conversation with cutting-edge technology. The AI Lab for Education is a hands-on, innovation-driven laboratory focused on applying AI to enhance learning and teaching.
Our research is intentionally interdisciplinary, exploring how AI intersects with:
Ethics and Law
Education and Linguistics
Diplomacy and Global Security
Health and Well-being
CamTech scholars are shaping global conversations — with invitations to speak at the
Nishan Forum on World Civilizations
, the Pontifical Academy of Social Sciences, and other international platforms.
At CamTech, we believe the future of AI must be guided by wisdom, not just code.
We are building a new kind of university — one that inspires ethical leadership, regional innovation, and global impact. Join us. Lead the AI revolution — with vision, values, and purpose.

Empowering Education, Inspiring Futures
Through the KMH Foundation, we believe education changes lives. By empowering youth and expanding access to higher education, we’re opening doors to opportunity, innovation, and a brighter Cambodia. ISI Group is more than a business—it’s a thriving ecosystem of progress, with sister companies including ISI Steel, ISI SEZ, Urbanland, Brown Coffee, ISI Tower, AIA Stadium, K-Mall, and more—each contributing to sustainable growth and social impact.
CamTech University proudly recognizes Mr. Kang Leng, CEO of ISI Group and our Founding Board Member, for his passionate commitment to education and his belief in building a better future through knowledge. Together, we are shaping the next generation of leaders
The Overseas Cambodian Investment Corporation (OCIC) is a leading developer driving Cambodia’s progress through key infrastructure and urban development projects, including Koh Pich, Koh Norea, Connexion Building, Chroy Changvar Satellite City, Techo International Airport, Canadia Tower, and more. OCIC’s vision goes beyond real estate—it actively supports the education sector by empowering young Cambodians with access to higher education and entrepreneurship opportunities through CamTech University.
A proud moment for CamTech University as we honor Mr. Thierry Tea — Vice President of OCIC, Vice Chairman of the Federation of Cambodian Startups, and Member of the Board of Directors, CamTech University — for his visionary support in advancing education and inspiring the next generation of Cambodian leaders.
